Here we address both issues for stabilizer codes. First, for Pauli noise generated by independent Bernoulli events, we prove that the Rényi-$n$ coherent information is nondecreasing in $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$. This follows from a general theorem: if independent random bits are mapped linearly to a fine label $T$ and a coarse label $C$, then the Rényi entropy difference $H_n(C)-H_n(T)$ is nondecreasing in $n$.
