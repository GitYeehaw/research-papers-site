From random initial membrane motions, a lone swimmer converges to a traveling membrane wave. In a pair, the stroke of a swimmer affects its neighbor far more than the neighbor affects its own best stroke. For swimmers in line, this influence decays as $1/\ell$ with their separation $\ell$, whereas the incentive to respond decays as $1/\ell^2$.
