Building on the Schur-Weyl framework of Schmiedt, Jensen, and Schlemmer, we extend this approach to molecular geometries whose rovibrational motion realizes cyclic and dihedral permutation subgroups. By combining the Schur-Weyl decomposition of the $n$-spin Hilbert space with the Kraśkiewicz-Weyman-Adin-Roichman major-index branching rule for the restriction $S_N \downarrow C_m$, we obtain a fully combinatorial method for determining nuclear-spin symmetry species and statistical weights. Each standard Young tableau corresponds to a definite cyclic character determined by its major index, with reflections yielding the associated dihedral symmetry, so that nuclear-spin species follow directly from tableau data without projection operators or case-specific constructions.
