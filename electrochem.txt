However, experimental studies often report ion densities orders of magnitude above this limit, whose physical origin remains incompletely explained in terms of transport layer (TL) properties. In this work, the capacitance of the TLs is identified to be the fundamental bottleneck: the maximum quantifiable ion density is limited to the charge that can accumulate at the perovskite/TL interfaces, so that TIC most often depends more strongly on TL properties than on the ionic properties of the perovskite.
