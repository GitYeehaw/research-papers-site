In Plaquette, a hardware error model is specified once, as Kraus operators, Hamiltonian-Lindblad dynamics, or an experimentally reconstructed quantum channel, and is compiled automatically into the exact or approximate representation required by each of four sampler classes: stabilizer sampling for Pauli noise, the new XPauli sampler for leakage and environment sectors, near-Clifford samplers for coherent errors, and full-state simulation for exact reference calculations.
