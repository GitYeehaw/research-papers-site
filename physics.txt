We exploit the curl-mode lensing reconstruction released as part of the Atacama Cosmology Telescope (ACT) Data Release~6 (DR6), based on five seasons of temperature and polarization data covering $9400~\mathrm{deg}^2$ of sky, to set new constraints on the dimensionless string tension $Gμ$ and the inter-commutation (reconnection) probability $P$. Modelling the string-induced curl power spectrum within the velocity-dependent one-scale framework, we obtain a $2σ$ upper bound on the combination $GμP^{-1}\le 3.5\times 10^{-5}$ in the small-$P$ regime, and $Gμ\le 5.0\times 10^{-5}$ at $2σ$ assuming the canonical Nambu-Goto value $P=1$. These represent the tightest constraints on cosmic strings derived from the curl-mode CMB lensing power spectrum to date.
