We introduce InterMimicGen, a self-evolving motion-imitation framework in which robot motion data and a tracking policy improve each other. First, we consolidate motion-captured human-object interaction datasets and retarget them into humanoid robot references while preserving whole-body coordination and dexterous hand-object relationships.
